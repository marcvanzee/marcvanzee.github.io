## About me

I am an AI research engineer with over a decade of experience building and maintaining large-scale codebases for foundational models. I currently work on mulitmodal efforts at Microsoft AI, including [MAI-Image-1](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-image-1-debuting-in-the-top-10-on-lmarena/) and internal video models. Before that, I contributed to [Imagen 3](https://deepmind.google/technologies/imagen-3/) (text-to-image),  [Veo 1 and 2](https://deepmind.google/technologies/veo/) (text-to-video) at DeepMind. Earlier in my career, I led neural network design efforts at Google Brain, serving as the Tech Lead of [Flax](https://github.com/google/flax). I also did quite a bit of theoretical research in knowledge representation & reasoning, and compositionality in language.

Throughout my career, I've worked across diverse areas of AI, collaborating with researchers and developers from various backgrounds and cultures. I thrive in dynamic environments, wearing multiple hats, and I’m passionate about fostering a positive and inclusive work culture where everyone feels empowered.
